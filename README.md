# GasFinally
Finally working on the GAS after exploring every bit of UE5, till now I have implemented the base of the game and the development goes on and updating everyday.
The game has now :-
Gameplay Effects, Gameplay Ability, NPC and the inventory system.
Player can attack npcs, get exp from them, take damage from them, kill them.
Player can pickup items like posion or health that can apply an effect on him.
Player has 5 slots to use.

AI use state trees to move around the area
AI when attacked responds by attacking and followng the player
AI loose interest in the player when not in sight
